Getting started

Tell us how you currently record sales and expenses, the period you need help with, and the reports you want. We’ll agree the records needed and the reporting scope before you share documents.

For your privacy, don’t send bank statements, payroll files, IDs, or other sensitive records by ordinary email. We’ll agree on a suitable way to share any documents after discussing the work.
