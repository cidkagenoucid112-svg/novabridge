Accounting Software Setup & Training

Set up a practical bookkeeping workflow in the accounting software that fits your business, then learn how to use it day to day.

Request a quote

Service availability and requirements vary by country. We’ll confirm local accounting, payroll, tax, and company-registration requirements for your location. Work that requires a local professional or registration will be handled with an appropriately qualified partner where needed.

What’s included

  • Initial setup of a chart of accounts and business details
  • Import or entry of opening balances and agreed records
  • Setup of customers, suppliers, products, and recurring entries where relevant
  • Practical training for the owner or staff on everyday tasks

Who it’s for

Owners and small teams moving from paper or spreadsheets to accounting software, or looking to make better use of a system they already have.

What you receive

  • An agreed configured account and bookkeeping workflow
  • A handover checklist and training session
  • A list of unresolved data or setup questions for the business to address

How pricing works

A one-time setup fee based on the software, number of accounts and users, data migration, and training needs. Software subscriptions and third-party fees are paid separately by the client.

Tell us what you need and we’ll confirm the scope, timeline, and fee before work begins.

Getting started

Tell us which system you use or are considering—such as Zoho Books, QuickBooks, or Xero—how much history you need moved, and who needs training. We’ll confirm product and plan availability before quoting.

For your privacy, don’t send bank statements, payroll files, IDs, or other sensitive records by ordinary email. We’ll agree on a suitable way to share any documents after discussing the work.
